Listen candidate 0.2.2 (4): submission and delivery pending. Match the final build/source commit; validation is recorded separately.

Rhea RF Spanner is an acoustic observatory. LIVE defaults to Listen: local microphone levels/spectrum with no app-generated tone. Tone experiment requires separate output agreement. DEMO is silent and synthetic. No camera, AR/LiDAR, RF acquisition, wireless scanning, external-device input, or measured spatial radar image.

Review navigation:
1. Launch: LIVE / Listen is idle. Permission is not requested on launch. Privacy & method is available without microphone access.
2. On a physical iPhone with built-in microphone input, tap Start listening. On first use, grant iOS permission; approval returns to idle. Tap Start listening again deliberately. Listen does not require Tone agreement or construct a tone source. Its recording session may interrupt other playback on this phone.
3. Observe LIVE · LISTEN, RMS in dBFS, peak in linear full-scale units, and Waterfall. Full audio spans the 0–24 kHz digital analysis band at validated 48 kHz input; it is not a microphone-response or sound-pressure calibration. The 18–22 kHz display range does not generate a tone. Listen has no carrier, phase, displacement, or reference controls.
4. Stop: Listen history is frozen and labelled as stopped data. Listen is not saved to Replay. A new run or mode/acquisition change clears displayed history.
5. Select DEMO and tap Record. This uses synthetic samples without microphone permission, an audio session, or speaker output. Try Field, Waterfall, and display controls. Stop, select Replay, and scrub retained DEMO telemetry. Replay contains Tone/DEMO captures only and preserves source labels.
6. Return to LIVE and explicitly select Tone experiment. Use low system volume, keep the speaker away from ears, and obtain nearby people's agreement. Agree in the app, then tap Record. A first permission grant requires another Record action. This mode plays 18–22 kHz in 500 Hz steps; it is not guaranteed inaudible. Carrier changes establish a new reference without resetting the 60-second budget. Display controls do not change acquisition.
7. Open Diagnostics during or after a run. Check input, captured/processed frames, spectra, and GPU counts. Listen has zero source/transmit counts and no tone fit. App tone enabled is the current app gate, distinct from retained configuration; neither proves physical sound emission.
8. Inspect JSON report previews a local snapshot. Copy Diagnostic Dump is the explicit copy action: local-only, excluded from Handoff, expiring after one hour. No report is uploaded automatically.

No app account, accessory, or backend is required. LIVE requires a physical device; Simulator supports silent DEMO and Replay. Unsupported routes/formats are explained. LIVE stops at 60 seconds, on inactivity, relevant audio interruptions/route/service events, or detected input/processing stalls. Privacy & method also stops acquisition. Returning never restarts it automatically.

Raw microphone audio is processed in volatile memory, not saved or uploaded. Up to 2,048 derived Tone/DEMO records plus bounded spectra remain in memory for Replay; no file export or persistence is provided. Diagnostics retains up to 128 events. Its optional report contains scalar summaries, counts, app/build and generic OS/model details, audio format/route types, relative intervals, and error identifiers. It excludes raw samples, spectrum arrays, images, location, route names/UIDs, account/network/device identifiers, and absolute host/uptime clocks. A clipboard copy can outlive the app; users choose where to paste it.

No speech transcription, identification, tracking, or background recording. Tone quality/displacement are conditional models, not calibrated SNR or measured range. False color is not temperature.
