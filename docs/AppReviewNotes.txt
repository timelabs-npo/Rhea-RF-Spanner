Review notes for the 0.2.1 (3) diagnostic candidate. Match these notes to the exact submitted build; delivery and review status are recorded separately.

Rhea RF Spanner is an acoustic phase observatory. LIVE emits a selected 18–22 kHz tone in 500 Hz steps through the built-in speaker and analyzes microphone samples at a verified 48 kHz rate. DEMO is silent and synthetic. Replay retains the original source label. This version does not acquire RF signals, scan wireless traffic, use ARKit/LiDAR, or form a measured spatial radar image.

Review navigation:
1. Launch normally. The app starts idle and does not request microphone permission.
2. Open Privacy & method without granting access.
3. Select DEMO and use the central Record control. This runs the processing/display path without microphone permission, an audio session, or speaker output. Try Field, Waterfall, gain, persistence, and palettes. In Spectrum range, compare Full audio (0–24 kHz) with Carrier (18–22 kHz). Field is a synthetic illustration; Waterfall is computed from synthetic samples. Range selection changes only the display.
4. Stop, select Replay, and play or scrub the retained telemetry. The source remains DEMO. No raw audio is recorded.
5. For LIVE, use a physical iPhone with its built-in audio route, select low system volume, keep the speaker away from ears, and obtain the agreement of people nearby. The tone is not guaranteed inaudible.
6. Agree to the selected tone and microphone analysis, then tap Record. On first use, grant the iOS permission. Permission approval returns to idle; tap Record again deliberately.
7. Observe the active source/frequency, input level, and Stop control. Changing carrier establishes a new phase-reference epoch. Display gain/persistence/range do not change the sample source or the fit. An unavailable phase can coexist with nonzero microphone input; Full audio Waterfall helps inspect sound outside the carrier band. Full audio is not a passive acquisition mode: LIVE still emits the selected carrier.
8. Open the menu's Diagnostics & copy report during or after a run. Inspect actual callback/processed-frame counts, input statistics, and rendering completion counts/rates. These are observed activity, not an assumed screen refresh rate. A running audio session alone is not treated as proof that samples are arriving.
9. Inspect JSON report previews a snapshot locally. Copy Diagnostic Dump explicitly copies it to the local clipboard, excluded from Handoff and expiring after one hour. The app does not send the report automatically.

No app account, login, accessory, or backend is required. LIVE is unavailable in Simulator; silent DEMO and Replay are available there. Unsupported audio routes/formats produce an explanation.

Microphone samples remain in volatile memory and are not recorded. Up to 2,048 derived 64-byte slices and bounded spectrum companions are retained in memory for Replay; no raw-audio/Replay file export or persistent library is provided. Diagnostics retains up to 128 operational events in memory and can copy a separate aggregate JSON report when requested. It includes app/build, generic OS/model, audio format/route types/volume, relative intervals, counters, input/analysis summaries, rendering status, and sanitized error codes. It excludes raw samples, spectrum arrays, images, location, route names/UIDs, account/network/device identifiers, and absolute system-uptime or host-clock values. The clipboard copy can outlive the app; users choose where to paste it. No report is uploaded automatically.

There is no speech transcription, identification, analytics SDK, advertising, tracking, remote configuration, downloaded code, or background recording. The microphone may receive surrounding speech as part of the acoustic signal.

LIVE has a 60-second limit. Leaving the active app, an interruption, route change, or audio-service event stops it. Privacy & method also stops it. Returning never automatically restarts LIVE. Explicit frequency changes during a run preserve the existing time budget.

Tone/residual quality is a model-fit statistic, not calibrated SNR or proof of a reflected target. Phase-derived displacement is a conditional model, not measured range. Invalid samples and gaps invalidate the reference; recovery establishes a new zero. The transmit amplitude of 0.02 digital full scale is not a sound-pressure calibration. False-color palettes do not measure temperature.
