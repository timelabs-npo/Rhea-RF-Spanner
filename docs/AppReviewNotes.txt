Rhea RF Spanner is an acoustic phase experiment for a physical iPhone. It emits a fixed 20 kHz tone through the built-in speaker and analyzes microphone samples at an actual 48 kHz sample rate. This version does not acquire radio-frequency signals, access the baseband, scan wireless traffic, or form a radar image.

Review steps:
1. Launch the app. It starts idle and does not request microphone permission on launch.
2. Open "Privacy & method"; this information is available without microphone access.
3. Use the built-in speaker and microphone, disconnect external audio routes, select a low device volume, and keep the speaker away from ears. Obtain the agreement of people nearby; the tone is not guaranteed to be inaudible.
4. Enable the agreement toggle and tap "Start experiment". On first use, grant iOS microphone permission. Granting permission returns to idle: tap "Start experiment" again when ready.
5. Observe the active microphone/tone banner, remaining-seconds counter, and numeric estimates. Tap "Stop experiment" at any time.

No app account, login credentials, external accessory, or backend is required. Live acoustic experiments are unavailable in the simulator. Unsupported routes/formats produce an explanation.

Samples and current measurements remain in volatile memory. Application code does not save audio files or measurement histories, transcribe speech, identify people, upload audio/telemetry, or include analytics, advertising, remote configuration, or background recording. The microphone may also receive surrounding speech; it is processed only as part of the acoustic input.

Audio is capped at 60 seconds of sampled data, with an automatic session timeout. Leaving the active app, an interruption, route change, or audio-service event stops the experiment. Opening "Privacy & method" also stops it. Returning never automatically restarts it.

The wave is explicitly an abstract visualization. Relative phase is a fitted estimate; displacement is a conditional phase model, not independently measured range. A strong tone does not establish a reflected target. Signal loss or a capture gap invalidates the reference; a subsequent valid estimate establishes a new zero. The fixed 0.02 digital amplitude is not a calibrated sound-pressure level.
